About

I'm Tejesh. I work on data and AI systems around equity research, and "AI" now shows up in every annual report I see. The SEC has started fining companies for overstating their AI, and its letters to companies mostly ask one thing: be specific. Whether a sentence is specific enough to check turned out to be something you can measure.

Every hour a small job on AWS checks the SEC for new annual reports from these companies, pulls out every sentence that mentions AI, and marks it up with a model trained on 252 sentences I labeled by hand. A filing shows up here a few minutes after it's filed.

The model is right about 7 times in 10 on the labels that matter, so read the numbers as a strong hint, not a verdict, and click through to the filing. Code, labels and a longer write-up: github.com/Tejesh18/ai-hype-checker.
